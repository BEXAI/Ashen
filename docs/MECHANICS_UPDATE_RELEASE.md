## Changes and preserved contracts

The engine owns a fixed simulation clock, resolved motion paths and chronologically resolved combat events. Rendering and feedback consume that state. Pure helpers cover timing/actions, swept collision, locomotion, contact outcomes, boss patterns and hazards; grounded death, camera clearance and bounded feedback integrate with the existing actors. Floor-hazard numbers have a separate above-hero anchor so a floor contact cannot hide the number behind the legs; sparks retain the resolved floor point. Reduce Motion also suppresses the production red damage overlay without changing authoritative damage or hit stop.

| Contract | Before | Implemented behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Simulation and input | Variable update per render; public action edges mutate immediately | 1/60 s ticks, ≤100 ms admission and six opportunities per frame; one tick-stamped command drain; discarded overload reported |
| Hit stop and playtime | Approx. 35 ms stop, including shielded contact | Accepted melee damage requests two frozen opportunities, maximum rather than sum; admitted foreground playtime includes freeze, excludes pause/background/discarded time |
| Movement | Immediate velocity, endpoint/axis collision, soft approach separation | Acceleration/braking, swept circles, recorded slide paths, immediate attack speed caps, deterministic living-body separation and bounded swept recoil |
| Solid masonry | Floor-union limits omitted projecting pillars, doorway posts and end-wall thickness | Rendering, movement and obstruction share solid envelopes; overhead lintels stay traversable below; safe saved-spawn validation also rejects masonry intersections |
| Contact | Articulated samples used final roots | Attacker and target sampled on their resolved paths at contact time; endpoint body corrections cannot create earlier hits |
| Dodge | Steerable and protected throughout; spawn reused dodge motion | Direction locked; 14 units/s for .30 s then .15 s deceleration, 5.25 unobstructed travel; protection [.05,.30); separate one-second spawn protection without a speed boost |
