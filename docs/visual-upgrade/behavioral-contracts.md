# Behavioral boundaries

Presentation work may change lighting, animation blending, material binding, diagnostics and adaptive quality. It must preserve one authoritative combat clock and root movement owner, active weapon contacts within 0.04 m, all 15 semantic Sovereign joints and 13 clips (Warden has 13 joints). Locomotion and cape curves may be deliberately improved; obsolete full-file/channel freezes are not acceptance criteria.

Progress stays version 1. Graphics preferences are validated device-local data, never a save payload or authorization input. The opaque guest cookie and trusted host identity, D1 parameterized queries, Origin checks, 16 KiB cap, runId/revision conflicts and SaveQueue remain behavioral requirements.
