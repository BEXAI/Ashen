# A8 roster acceptance reconciliation

**Finding: no new mechanics-specific roster blocker identified.** Existing all-roster asset/contact checks, retained grounding acceptance, presentation tests and the newly guarded install boundary address the concrete risks in A8. No additional regression or asset rebuild is justified by this review. This finding does not turn sampled asset renders or representative browser captures into a new complete mobile/HD/fallback visual pass.

This was a read-only review of the current `Ashen` checkout, the current 32 delivery files, archived source/grounding/pose evidence and existing browser captures. No source files, models or browser state were changed; no broad suite was rerun. Test coverage below was inspected in source. The integrating owner’s final suite run remains the execution authority. `evidence.json` binds audited source/test files and all model hashes to the reviewed checkout state.

## Grounding decisions already resolved

The previous Lion back-sword lift and Ogre club-supported corpse were fixed by body-only grounding masks; the final accepted records describe those repairs. Sage’s pinned boots were restored to leg motion. The final Goblin hip sheet, Skeleton sword fan, staff bends, Reaper robe spikes and Dragon wing ribbons were repaired before their accepted hashes were recorded. Current files match those hashes; they are not the earlier rejected deliveries.

Some held/stowed equipment can enter the floor during death, intentionally excluded from body grounding so it cannot lift the body. The new boss overlay retains the measured source tolerances: about −6.8 cm at the imported claw/chain and −4.5 cm at fallback boots in the sampled full-mesh sequence, with the real slam surface point reaching approximately +3.5 cm at impact. These are documented bounded intersections, not evidence of a newly drifting actor root or an invisible extended weapon. No claim of perfect foot planting, zero garment overlap or continuous mesh-floor collision is made.
