## Concrete risks to address in the future plan

These are code-derived engineering risks or explicit design decisions to make, not video conclusions. They do not all require changing existing behavior.

1. **Clock policy under load.** Sustained 10 FPS advances at most 0.5 simulation seconds per real second because excess elapsed time is discarded. Existing strike travel tests exercise explicit timesteps, not the whole engine's clock cap. Decide whether slow motion is intentional. If it is not, separate a bounded fixed-step simulation from rendering, define the maximum catch-up budget, and preserve explicit hit-stop behavior. Test identical input traces at 30/60/120 FPS and with 100–250 ms stalls; include cooldowns, stamina, travel, input buffering, pause, and resume.
2. **Dodge duration and protection are coupled.** The timer is decremented before movement and damage checks in the frame; the last partial dodge interval is not integrated separately. Its displacement/last protected frame can therefore depend on timestep boundaries. Respawn protection also shares the dodge movement branch. Specify direction lock versus steering, separate spawn protection if appropriate, and test exact boundary crossings and elapsed-time travel at several rates.
3. **Movement collision is not a general sweep.** Endpoint floor tests plus X-then-Z sliding work for current short steps, but do not guarantee collision along a long future dash, knockback, or root-motion displacement. The dedicated throne/gate crossing checks do not generalize to all geometry. Before adding faster moves, test long trajectories across room gaps, thin walls, diagonal corners, seals, and the throne; use a swept character shape or bounded movement substeps if the new requirements need them.
4. **Enemy bodies can overlap.** Soft separation is skipped at attack distance and when exactly coincident; the hero has no actor-body rejection. If the video references require body pressure, crowd blocking, or knockback, define a kinematic crowd constraint explicitly. Test two enemies at the same position, opposing pushes at a doorway, a player dodging into a group, and differing Ogre/Spider/Goblin sizes. Avoid silently applying generic rigid-body dynamics to the imported skins.
5. **Subsampled weapon animation is not a full motion sweep.** The engine moves the actor to this frame's final root position before sampling all earlier weapon poses, and targets are tested at their current centers. This preserves the active window but does not reconstruct moving actor/target trajectories within that frame. For faster future combat, test simultaneous lateral crossing, attack lunges, knockback during contact, and wall adjacency; interpolate root/target state per sample or add a swept contact method if required.
6. **Camera collision has no volume.** Double-checking the target-to-camera ray protects its center; it does not guarantee near-plane corners clear walls or new props. Test narrow doorways, inside corners, pitch extremes, sprint turns, portrait/landscape changes, and teleport/respawn. Add a camera sphere/near-plane sweep only if observed clipping warrants it.
7. **Vertical mechanics would be new scope.** `height()`, spawn validation, save position fields, camera target, hit capsules, and attack roots all assume flat XZ play. Slopes, jumps, ledges, stairs, falling, or ragdolls require a coherent ground/support model rather than only replacing animation clips. [model.ts:15](/Users/nathaniel/Documents/ChatGPT/Knight/Ashen/app/game/model.ts:15) records X/Z but no vertical state. Preserve the old save contract unless a reviewed migration is explicitly required.
