## Animation and asset contracts that mechanics changes must respect

- `app/game/character-skins.ts:5` defines `ActorVisual`: absolute strike/locomotion/reaction sampling, presentation lifecycle, blade segment, optional muzzle, LOD and disposal. `app/game/combat-animation.ts:36` delegates to imported animation when available and retains a procedural fallback; `:63` exposes weapon endpoints.
- `app/game/roster-assets.ts:14` defines model height/yaw, contact phases, embedded equipment/socket flags, and grounding curves. Installation at `:34` requires 12 canonical clips, skinned geometry and `ContactBase`/`ContactTip`; instances clone independent skeletons. Embedded flags prevent duplicated weapons or overwritten real sockets.
- `app/game/roster-assets.ts:64` maps engine windup/active/recovery into native clip contact fractions. Gameplay strike duration and native clip duration are intentionally separate. The adapter samples absolute times (`:53`), restores presentation before collision sampling, and evaluates actual world-space endpoints/muzzles (`:75`). Merely changing phase durations need not retime or regenerate GLBs, but contact behavior must still be retested.
- Grounding at `app/game/roster-assets.ts:62` interpolates per-clip floor curves. Clip, rest-pose, root-transform or geometry changes may require regenerated grounding and contact-phase measurements. A render-only root correction must not silently move authoritative weapon contacts.
- `app/game/roster-presentation.ts:16`–`:30` restores the authored pose before samples and blends only for rendering. Active contact remains unblended. `app/game/character-assets.ts:31` and `:56` retain a separate legacy adapter; test it as well as the current recovered roster.

## Preserve safety and data contracts; decide balance deliberately

Preserve unless the new specification explicitly supersedes them with an equally clear contract: finite transforms; independent actor skeletons; single-hit accounting and reward deduplication; intended target caps; wall/gate obstruction; actual equipment contacts; no queued input surviving cancellation or lifecycle loss; render-only effects not changing authoritative damage; graphics quality not changing rules; and existing save ownership, revision and run-ID protections.

The four-strike order, exact timings/costs, cancel windows, lunge distances, facing/aim assist, dodge duration and direction, hit-pause scope, regeneration rates, capsule dimensions, enemy range/spacing/interrupt policy, and hitscan-versus-projectile choice are **current design choices**, not immutable safety invariants. They may intentionally change after the videos are reviewed. Record each change and its acceptance test rather than forcing new mechanics to reproduce obsolete balance fixtures.

## Persistence and migration requirements

`app/game/model.ts:15` defines a strict version-1 progress schema, with fixed quest/enemy identifiers, health/mana, equipment ranks, flasks, hero, position and progression. Stamina, cooldowns, active swing and surviving-enemy partial HP are currently transient. Changing only transient mechanics may not require a save schema migration.

Adding persistent resources, equipment/stat fields or new progress identifiers does require an explicit migration/default policy. `app/api/progress/route.ts:15` currently loads stored JSON without a version migration; PUT validates the strict schema at `:23`, and upgrades parse stored progress at `:54`. Apply migration consistently before load and upgrade validation, retain old-version golden fixtures, and preserve user ownership plus conditional revision/run-ID updates. Do not silently reset existing journeys. `app/game/Game.tsx:58` and `app/game/mobile-runtime.ts:70` own serialized save behavior and conflict handling. Combat remains client-simulated; the current server validates progress shape and purchase/revision operations, not every battle event.
