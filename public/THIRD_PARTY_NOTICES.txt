as original bpy geometry and procedural appearance in user-owned Higgsfield

records are preserved in assets-source/props/v1/sconce and
assets-source/props/v2/{shrine,architecture}. Exact current runtime URLs,
checksums, source/recipe mappings and limitations are recorded in
assets-source/props/v2/release-ledger.json. That ledger is provenance evidence,
not a grant of rights to unrelated catalog assets or a claim of exclusive copyright.
